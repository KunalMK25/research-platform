# Research Plan: EV Adoption in India

## Subtopics

### 1. Current State and Market Trends
- Recent statistics on EV sales and market penetration in India.
- Breakdown of adoption by vehicle segment (2-wheelers, 3-wheelers, passenger cars, commercial vehicles).
- Regional variations in adoption rates across different Indian states.

### 2. Government Policies and Incentives
- Analysis of central government schemes (e.g., FAME II, PLI scheme).
- Overview of state-specific EV policies and subsidies.
- Tax benefits and non-financial incentives for consumers and manufacturers.

### 3. Charging Infrastructure and Battery Ecosystem
- Current status and growth rate of public and private EV charging networks.
- Emerging trends in battery swapping technology and infrastructure.
- Domestic battery manufacturing capabilities and supply chain for battery materials.

### 4. Supply Chain and Local Manufacturing
- The role of major Indian and global OEMs in the domestic EV market.
- Progress in localizing EV component manufacturing (motors, controllers, etc.).
- Challenges related to importing critical minerals and components.

### 5. Consumer Perception and Economic Viability
- Key barriers to adoption (e.g., range anxiety, high upfront costs).
- Availability of financing options for EVs compared to ICE vehicles.
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) analysis and overall consumer awareness.
